Open a general arrangement

Drop a file here, or press Open GA. It can be a PDF, a DXF or SVG drawing, or a picture (JPG, PNG). The app reads the particulars, finds the views, and suggests decks, bulkheads and tank blocks from the lines of the drawing. You check each suggestion, then save a file for the DBB editor.

It works best on vector drawings. Everything stays in this page.

OpenCV settings how the lines of a picture or a view are read
Drag to turn, wheel to zoom.
Levels are the horizontal lines of the profile, in metres above the baseline. Tick the decks of the superstructure: a block is made between each tick and the next, and from the main deck up to the first.
Checks against the particulars. The drawing is low resolution, so small differences are normal.
